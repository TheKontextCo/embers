# Community code of conduct

Embers welcomes rigorous, kind collaboration. We want people of every
background and level of experience to be able to examine the evidence, report
what failed, and improve the project without harassment or intimidation.

## Expected behaviour

- Discuss the work, its evidence, and its impact rather than attacking people.
- Make room for questions, corrections, and different levels of familiarity.
- Respect privacy. Never publish another person's notes, logs, identity, or
  security report without their explicit permission.
- Accept a maintainer's request to stop disruptive or unsafe behaviour.

## Unacceptable behaviour

Harassment, discrimination, threats, sexualized attention, deliberate
intimidation, doxxing, and sustained personal attacks are not welcome in any
project space. That includes issues, pull requests, discussions, and private
project communication.

## Enforcement

Report conduct concerns privately through the repository owner's contact route
on [The Kontext Co GitHub profile](https://github.com/TheKontextCo). Do not put
private evidence in a public issue. Maintainers may edit or remove content,
close or lock threads, reject contributions, or temporarily or permanently ban
participation when needed to protect the community.

Reports will be handled as privately as practical. Retaliation against someone
who reports a concern is itself a violation.
