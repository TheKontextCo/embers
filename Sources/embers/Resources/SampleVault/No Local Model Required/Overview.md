# No Local Model Required

An ordinary Markdown folder already tells a story: which notes belong together,
what they are called, and which tasks remain unfinished. Embers reads that
structure and turns it into context you can bring up by voice.

Say “local model” to bring up this context. Those words appear in its title, so
matching them needs no language-model proposal. The notes and tasks come from the
files themselves.

## Local Model

People do not always use the words they wrote. When Apple's on-device language
models are available, Embers uses them to suggest other ways to refer to a
context. It checks those suggestions against the source material before making
them available for voice matching.

Local Intelligence explains how those suggestions become reusable matching
rules. Deterministic Core explains what remains under the control of ordinary
code. Context Lens lets you inspect the boundary between them.
